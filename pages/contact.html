Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is ShroomScan?

ShroomScan is a research project developing an AI-based system for mushroom disease detection and classification. It combines machine learning with agricultural science.

❓ How can I use the system?

The system can be accessed through our web platform. Users can upload mushroom images for disease analysis and receive real-time classification results with recommendations.

❓ Is the system available for download?

Information about system availability, licensing, and deployment options can be found in our project documents or by contacting the team directly.

❓ How accurate is the detection?

Our model achieves high accuracy rates on diverse datasets. Specific accuracy metrics and performance details are available in our research documents and publications.

❓ Can I collaborate on this project?

We welcome collaboration inquiries from researchers, institutions, and organizations. Please contact our supervisors or project leads for partnership opportunities.

❓ Where is the source code available?

Information about code repository, licensing, and open-source contributions can be obtained by contacting the development team directly.
