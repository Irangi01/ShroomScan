About Our Team

Meet the talented researchers and developers behind ShroomScan

Project Overview

ShroomScan is a comprehensive research project aimed at developing an intelligent machine learning-based system for early detection and classification of mushroom diseases. The project brings together a dedicated team of computer science students and experienced supervisors to create a practical solution that can benefit agricultural practitioners worldwide.

Our team leverages expertise in machine learning, web development, database design, and agricultural science to build a robust, scalable, and user-friendly platform for mushroom disease detection and management.
