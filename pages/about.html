About Our Team

Meet the researchers behind Smart Mushroom Incubation System (AIMS)

Project Overview

Our project develops a Smart Mushroom Incubation System that combines IoT sensors, machine learning, and automated controls to enhance oyster mushroom cultivation in Sri Lanka. This innovative system addresses critical challenges faced by small-scale farmers through real-time environmental monitoring, predictive analytics, and early detection of pests and contamination.

The system integrates four key intelligent components:

  • ML-Based Fruiting Prediction: Forecasts optimal fruiting timing using environmental data
  • Smart Pest Detection: Automated camera-based pest monitoring with instant alerts
  • Substrate Advisory System: Real-time pH and moisture management recommendations
  • Contamination Detection: CNN-based early contamination identification and prevention

Our team brings together expertise in machine learning, embedded systems, IoT integration, and agricultural science to create a practical, affordable solution that transforms mushroom farming from reactive to proactive, data-driven operations.

Project Innovations & Contributions

🤖

Integrated Smart System

First comprehensive system combining IoT monitoring, ML prediction, computer vision, and automated response for mushroom incubation in Sri Lankan farming context.

🌱

Sustainable Farming Impact

Transforms traditional farming practices into data-driven operations, reducing chemical use, minimizing crop losses, and enabling small-scale farmer scalability.

🔬

Multi-Component Approach

Addresses four critical farming challenges simultaneously: fruiting optimization, pest management, substrate health, and contamination prevention.

💡

Affordable Innovation

Designed specifically for rural, small-scale farmers with limited resources, ensuring cost-effectiveness and practical deployability across Sri Lanka.
